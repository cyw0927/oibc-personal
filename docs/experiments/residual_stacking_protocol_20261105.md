# Residual stacking: chronological experiment

Use previously produced, pre-lock out-of-fold predictions for two forecasts: independent site weather predictions aggregated by #990 group, and the existing v2 group-direct predictions. A third model learns their fixed 0.4/0.6 blend's group residual, normalized by the available cohort capacity; do not search mixture weights. Fit a small weighted HGB absolute-error regressor using only prior out-of-fold residuals, group/time and forecast-derived features. Price/k may weight historical training rows but current/future prices are not inputs to the residual predictor. Apply a nonnegative bound. No actual, availability count, measured weather, lag, future price, or validation labels may enter model features.

Two chronological checks: train APR 2024 OOF and evaluate AUG 2024 (same independent site-model-based proxy on both periods); train JUL 2024 OOF and evaluate OCT 2024, with both a proxy reference and the actual #1314 reference (FS2+L1 plus v2 group-direct). Training predictions must themselves have been generated by models trained before their OOF period. The July/October inputs satisfy the same temporal ordering from existing run metadata. APR/AUG generation's recorded fit cutoffs also precede their OOF periods. Match group, timestamp, actual and price against reference; reject missing or duplicate keys. Hold out the full target validation month from meta-model fitting.

Both destination periods and earlier experiments have been inspected previously, so this is exploratory, not an untouched test. APR has 174 sites and violates 20 MW; AUG has 250; JUL 224; OCT 252. Results apply only to fixed historical cohorts; no annual or official score is implied. All inputs stay pre-lock, no external data or leaderboard tuning. The evaluation references use historical team data only for comparison; use rights remain to be confirmed. Store generated data outside Git.
